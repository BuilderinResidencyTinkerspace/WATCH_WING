# Week 6

**Goal this week:** Get the Depron sheet into hand, lock in a final airframe design around it, and actually get the body built.

## What we did
- Depron finally arrived — pre-cut close to 5mm, exactly the material we should have started with, given everything the past couple weeks put us through.
- Decided to stop trying to custom-derive our own span/chord numbers and just build to the official Flite Test **Simple Scout** plan as-is, since its own published specs lined up well with what we actually needed:
  - Wingspan: 952mm
  - Wing area: 20.9 dm²
  - Dry weight: 425.2g, All-Up Weight: 535.8g
  - Wing loading: 25.6 g/dm²
  - Against our A2212 1400KV motor's ~900g thrust, that works out to roughly a 1.68:1 thrust-to-weight ratio — comfortably strong, with genuine room left over for our electronics payload (Pixhawk, GPS, Pi Camera).
- Also compared this against the Simple Storch as a possible alternative, but its stock AUW (~1005g) and wingspan (1460mm) were a clearly heavier, bigger class than our motor and payload were ever sized for — Scout was the right call.
- Started cutting and building the body — wings, fuselage, tail — traced straight off the printed Scout plan.
- Onam fell right in the middle of this stretch, and between family time, travel, and general festival stuff, actual hands-on build hours were a lot fewer than a normal week — this wasn't a full, uninterrupted week of building.
- Even so, we used the pockets of time we did have well, and the body actually got built during this Onam window, which felt like a genuine win given how limited the working hours were.

## Problems and blockers
- Onam vacation ate into a big chunk of available build time this week — progress happened in shorter bursts rather than steady daily work.

## Decisions
- Final airframe: stock Flite Test Simple Scout, built to plan, no custom scaling.
- Ruled out the Simple Storch as an alternative — too heavy and too large a wingspan class for our motor and payload.

## Next week
- Continue electronics bring-up in parallel (RC transmitter/receiver/ESC/motor/servo bench testing).
- Begin planning the fuselage/electronics bay modification needed to fit Pixhawk, GPS, and camera.
