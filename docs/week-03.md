# Week 3

**Goal this week:** Build the airframe (wings, fuselage, tail) using foam board.

## What we did
- Cut and assembled the wing panels over ~3 days.
- Weighed the finished wing set: 300 g at 1250 cm² of wing area.
- Cross-checked against design targets (component weight + motor thrust of ~900 g) and found both wing area and wing weight were significantly off.
- Traced the cause to a material mix-up: used PVC foam board ("Sunboard," sold locally for signage/hoardings) instead of true hobby-grade foam (Depron/EPP). Derived areal density from our own wing: 0.24 g/cm² — roughly 8x heavier than true Depron at the same thickness.
- Cross-checked our sizing against a published reference design using the same 2212 motor class (640mm length, 810mm span, ~495g flight weight, 4-channel/4-servo control) — confirmed our servo count and motor choice are correct.
- Recalculated target wing dimensions from first principles: settled on a 950mm span and a wing loading target of 40 g/dm² (deliberately mid-range — lighter/simpler to build than a max-forgiveness target, while staying well below typical trainer wing loading for a comfortable safety margin).
- Ran the numbers at a 600g total weight as safety target: wing area required ≈ 1500cm², chord ≈ 16cm at 950mm span. Thrust-to-weight at this range comes out to 1.5 with our existing motor — strong margin.

## Problems and blockers
- Original wing came in under-area (1250 cm² vs. target) and overweight (300 g), due to the calculation and  material issue above.
- At the original (incorrect) material and area, projected wing loading was ~84.6 g/dm² with an estimated stall speed of ~50 km/h — well outside acceptable range.
 - **Foam board" is an ambiguous term locally — PVC foam sheet (signage-grade) and true Depron/EPP (hobby-grade) are sold under similar names; we sourced the wrong one.**

## Decisions
- **Full wing rebuild**, not an extension of the current wings — sourcing true Depron/EPP this time.
- **Final structural targets locked in:**
  - Wingspan: **950 mm**
  - Chord: **~16 cm** (splitting the 600, 1500 cm² wing area)
  - Wing loading target: **40 g/dm²**
  - Estimated AUW: **600** (built-in safety margin over the bare component estimate)
  - Estimated stall speed at this loading: **~34.5 km/h** — comfortably below even a typical trainer's baseline
- **Tail surfaces will be rebuilt at matching scale:**
  - Horizontal stabilizer: **~220–300 cm²** (15–20% of wing area)
  - Vertical fin / rudder: **~130–150 cm²** (8–10% of wing area)
- Fuselage length can largely stay close to original plan (~640–680mm) — the wing was the mis-sized element, not the fuselage.
- Material will be verified before purchase: check cut-edge cross-section (solid = wrong material) and weigh a sample piece against known Depron density before committing to a full sheet.

## Next week
- Source correct Depron/EPP foam board for wings, fuselage, and tail.
- Cut wings to finalized 950mm span / ~16cm chord.
- Cut horizontal stabilizer and vertical fin/rudder to match the new wing area ratios.
- Re-weigh full airframe once rebuilt and confirm AUW lands in the 600 target range.
- Re-check wing loading and thrust-to-weight against actual measured weight before proceeding.
